# Direction: workshop pegboard

Toolshed should feel like a friendly neighborhood workshop, not a marketplace. The paper-cream background and deep pine green come from workbench and garden-shed materials. Safety orange is the only loud color. It is reserved for the "Lend a tool" button and small accents (avatar ring, active tab underline), so the primary action is the first thing the eye finds.

Type is a rounded system stack with heavy weights for greetings and headings. That keeps it warm and compact. Tool photos are flat, geometric silhouettes on tinted tiles.

Availability never relies on color alone. It pairs a dot or square marker with text ("Available now", "From Nov 22"). Text is at least 13 px and all text pairs reach at least 4.9:1 contrast. Chips, tabs and the button are at least 44 px tall.
