Direction: a warm neighborhood workshop, not a generic marketplace. Cream paper background and a deep forest green carry the brand; a single safety orange is reserved for the one thing we want people to do, "Lend a tool", so it is the only warm accent on screen.

A serif display face (Palatino/Georgia stack) for the greeting and section title gives a hand-lettered, workshop-sign feel against a rounded UI sans. Photo placeholders sit on a pegboard dot pattern with a simple line-drawn tool glyph, echoing a shed wall.

Availability uses a dot plus text in two accessible tones (green now, brown later), never colour alone. All text meets 4.5:1, targets are 44px or larger, and body text is at least 13px.
