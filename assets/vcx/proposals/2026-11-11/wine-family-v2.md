## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Produce ONE beautifully designed concept comparison board for a wine-inspired software family: VCX, Cellar, Blends, Brix. This is an exploratory presentation sheet, not production icon exports. Four equal large icon tiles arranged in a spacious 2x2 grid on warm off-white background, with a short exact product name below each tile. Landscape canvas, exquisite contemporary branding, consistent rounded geometry, tactile satin finish and shallow 3D relief. Sophisticated winemaking references must be unmistakable, avoiding generic software blocks and branching arrows. Different harmonious wine-world color per product; NOT an all-burgundy family.
Top left VCX: a deep midnight-plum rounded tile with a compact sculptural bunch of grapes, six pearl-lavender grape spheres arranged three-two-one and one minimal curved vine stem. Broad iconic silhouette; suggests independent grapes making a whole. Label "VCX".
Top right Cellar: a dark forest-green rounded tile with a warm oak-colored front-facing barrel emblem: softly rounded barrel silhouette, two broad vertical curved stave seams and two simple horizontal cream bands; minimal cellar-aging reference, no tiny wood grain, no doors or locks. Label "Cellar".
Bottom left Blends: a muted dusty-rose rounded tile, a single large flowing circular swirl of TWO contrasting wine liquids, deep garnet red and pale champagne gold, merging organically into one round swirling pool with an off-center open center. Visibly liquid with smooth meniscus and gentle silky highlights, like swirling red and white wines together; fluid curved flowing lobes, not arrows, not an infinity sign, not a yin-yang symbol with dots. Modern abstract wine blending emblem, no cup or goblet outline, no splashes or droplets outside it. Label "Blends".
Bottom right Brix: an amber-ochre rounded tile with a single large dark aubergine drop of grape must, three short evenly spaced ivory horizontal measurement ticks embedded along one inner edge of the drop. Smooth plump drop silhouette, scales symbolize sugar measurement and potential alcohol, not a water utility. No percent symbols or numeric values. Label "Brix".
Unify the set through tile proportions, roundness, visual weight and restrained soft tactile lighting; each symbol occupies roughly 65% of its tile. Icons clean and generous negative space. Labels in precise rounded dark sans-serif beneath tiles, legible exact text, no slogans, no explanations, no watermarks. Not childish, not photoreal wine bottles, not ornate winery heraldry. Contemporary distinctive software identities rooted in the wine universe.
