## Prompt del ábaco

Use case: logo-brand. Create a standalone proposed isotipo (symbol-only logo) for Pinot Index, a scoring engine that computes and accumulates results of blind wine tasting games. Concept A: a minimal abacus, conveying calculation and scoring, with grape-like counting beads as a subtle connection to Pinot.
The provided tintum brand image is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: match its sophisticated rounded inflated shapes, fine tactile silicone-rubber microtexture, rich burgundy #7B2335 and warm cream #F4EDE0. Do not copy its t symbol or include its text.
Design ONE iconic compact abacus in a rounded-square burgundy tile: a thick rounded cream inverted-U frame joined to a cream bottom bar, exactly two horizontal cream rods with ample space between them, exactly three large burgundy-red soft spherical beads total (two on upper rod, one on lower rod). The beads should be visibly lighter/brighter than tile for contrast, not camouflaged, with satin rubber shading. All geometry soft and rounded, weight and restraint matching tintum. The abacus should read in silhouette, frame thick enough for small sizes; minimal components, no tiny screws, ticks, numbers, wires or realistic wooden toy detail. Two rows suggest independent scoring series without depicting a chart.
Orthographic straight-on view, centered, no perspective tilt. Tile fills 85 percent of square canvas, mark occupies 70 percent of tile width. Very fine microtexture and restrained soft 3D volume, no rough dirt, no fake wear, no glossy metal. Transparent background outside tile, no external shadow. Only one icon, no wordmark, no labels, no mockup, no typography.

## Prompt de sumatoria

Use case: logo-brand. Standalone proposed isotipo for Pinot Index, an engine that calculates scores and accumulates results. Concept B: a beautifully rounded mathematical SUMMATION SYMBOL ∑. This signifies the aggregation of results, not a numerical rating or a promise about the exact algorithm.
Input image is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: reproduce the tintum family of rounded soft forms with fine tactile rubber microtexture, warm cream #F4EDE0 and rich wine burgundy #7B2335. Do not include tintum text or its t monogram.
Create a single compact icon with a burgundy rounded square tile and a warm cream summation ∑ symbol centered on it. The symbol must unequivocally read as a capital sigma summation operator: top horizontal from left to right, two diagonals meeting at the inward mid-height vertex on the LEFT/interior and opening on the right, bottom horizontal from left to right. Standard ∑ topology, smoothly rounded ends and corners with generous curves, sturdy consistent tube-like stroke, strong open negative spaces. NOT a Latin E, NOT a number 3, NOT a zigzag lightning bolt. The middle vertex points rightwards toward the center of the overall symbol (standard capital Sigma: upper left to central right to lower left). Maintain the standard recognizable ∑ glyph while softening all angular joints. Use the familiar Unicode glyph ∑ as exact geometry guide. Do not add dots, grapes, digits, equation decorations, glasses, a face or other objects.
Straight-on orthographic view. Restrained dimensional relief as if the cream glyph is molded silicone attached to a burgundy silicone tile. Fine even texture, soft studio light, tasteful shadows within tile, no dirt, no metallic shine. Tile occupies 85 percent of square canvas, glyph around 60 percent of tile width. Transparent outside rounded square, no cast shadow outside it. One icon only, no wordmark, no explanatory text, no mockup.
